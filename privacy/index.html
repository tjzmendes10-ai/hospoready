Privacy policy

What we collect, why we collect it, and how we look after it.

Last updated: 1 November 2026

The short version

  • We only collect what we need to run HospoReady and keep you informed about it.
  • We store it securely and we never sell your information.
  • You can ask us to delete your data at any time.

What we collect now

HospoReady hasn't launched yet. Right now we only collect what you give us when you join the waitlist:

  • your name
  • your email address
  • your venue name
  • your suburb
  • your state or territory
  • your venue type
  • which plan you're interested in

The free checklist and the demo wallet don't send anything to us. Anything you enter there stays in your own browser on your device.

What we'll collect after launch

Once HospoReady launches and you sign up, we'll also store what you choose to add to your account:

  • licence, permit and certificate details, such as names, numbers, issuing authorities and dates
  • photos of your documents
  • staff names and the dates of their certificates, such as RSA and Food Safety Supervisor

We'll update this policy before launch with more detail about how the full service handles your information.

Why we collect it

  • Waitlist details: to let you know when HospoReady launches, offer early access and understand which venues and states to support first.
  • Account details (after launch): to show your licences and certificates in one place, work out what's expiring, and send you reminders.

We won't use your details for anything unrelated to HospoReady.

How we store it

We store your information securely and limit access to the people who need it to run HospoReady. Waitlist submissions are processed and stored by Formspree, a form service, on our behalf.

We never sell your data

We don't sell, rent or trade your information, and we don't share it with advertisers.

Deleting your data

You can ask us to delete your information at any time, including removing you from the waitlist. Just email us at the address below and we'll take care of it.
