Running a venue means juggling a pile of paperwork

Your council, the NSW Government, industry bodies and licensing organisations each issue their own approvals, each with its own renewal date and rules. Nobody brings them together for you.

🏛️

Many issuers

Council approvals, NSW Food Authority, Liquor & Gaming NSW, music licensing bodies – all with separate portals and paperwork.

📅

Different dates

Some renew yearly, some every five years, and staff certificates expire one person at a time. It's easy to lose track.

⚠️

Costly to miss

A lapsed licence or certificate can mean fines, failed inspections or even losing the right to trade until it's sorted.
