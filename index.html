Running a venue means juggling a pile of paperwork

Your council, the NSW Government, industry bodies and licensing organisations each issue their own approvals, each with its own renewal date and rules. Nobody brings them together for you.

  • Many issuers. Separate portals, separate paperwork.
  • Different dates. Some yearly, some every five years, staff certificates one person at a time.
  • Costly to miss. Fines, failed inspections, or losing the right to trade until it's sorted.
