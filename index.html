Running a venue means juggling a pile of paperwork

Your council, your state or territory government, liquor and gaming regulators and industry bodies each issue their own approvals, each with its own renewal date and rules. And the rules differ from state to state and council to council. Nobody brings them together for you.

  • Many issuers. Separate portals, separate paperwork, different in every state.
  • Different dates. Some yearly, some every five years, staff certificates one person at a time.
  • Costly to miss. Fines, failed inspections, or losing the right to trade until it's sorted.
